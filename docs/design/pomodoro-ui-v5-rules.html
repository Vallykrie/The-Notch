Focus timer · two rules to settle before building

Building on v4, option 3 (Strip + walker). These are the two questions you raised. Each section has my recommendation, shown live.

1 · The long break has to scale with your break

A fixed 15-minute long break only makes sense for short breaks. With a 30-minute break, the "long" break would actually be shorter. My recommendation: no fixed number. The long break is 3× your break, rounded to 5 minutes, between 10 and 45 minutes. If your break is 15 minutes or more, there's no long break at all, because every break is already a long one, and the set simply repeats. The setup screen always shows what you'll get, in the line under the rulers, so it's never a surprise. For anyone who wants a fixed number, Settings → Focus has a Long break option: Auto (default), Off, or a fixed length.

2 · Sharing the notch with Media and Agents

How it works today (from LiveActivityLayout): the closed notch has a left and a right side, 40 pt each, and each side holds one icon. Media plus agents shows the artwork on the left and the agent sprite on the right. A volume or brightness change outranks everything, and a pinned crypto price replaces media and agents unless an agent needs you.

The focus timer adds a third live thing, and it's the only one that needs text (the countdown). My recommendation:

  • Focus always owns the right side. The countdown sits there with a thin 2 pt progress line under it (amber in focus, green on break). The timer is the one thing you started and are watching, so it gets a fixed home and never moves around.
  • The left side shows what else is live, following today's rules. If nothing else is live, it's the focus mascot filling up. Media shows the artwork, agents show the agent's own sprite in its status colour, and with both it shows the artwork and the sprite side by side.
  • Never two mascots. When agents are live, the one mascot on the left is the agent's and shows agent status. Focus progress moves to the line under the countdown, so a blob never means two things at once.
  • Both sides grow from 40 to 56 pt while focus is running. That's enough for the countdown, or for artwork plus sprite. Both sides stay the same width so the notch stays centred, which the current code requires.
  • Order of priority: a volume/brightness change (brief) > an agent asking for approval > focus > media / agents > crypto. A running focus session takes over from the pinned crypto price, and the price comes back after Reset.

Round end while an agent needs you

When a round ends, the notch normally drops open for 6 s with "Round done · Start break". If an agent's approval card is already open, the approval stays and the round-end message waits its turn. It shows once the approval is dealt with, or straight away if auto-start already began the break. Blocking an agent costs more than a late "time for a break".
