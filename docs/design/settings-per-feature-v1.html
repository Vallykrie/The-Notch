Settings, split per feature

Today all 14 controls share one 640×190 grid. These mockups split them by feature, give each feature an on/off switch (so someone who only uses agents can switch Media and Crypto off completely), and leave room for more settings. Everything below is clickable: switches, value fields, tabs, and the feature switches. Turning a feature off removes its tab and its shoulder from the notch below. Rows tagged NEW are settings that don't exist yet.

Rules that apply to every option: switching a feature off stops its monitor (no polling Music, no price feed), hides its tab and shoulder, and keeps its settings for when it's switched back on. If every feature is off, the notch still does the HUD and hover, and settings stay reachable from the gear. The intro could also ask "what will you use The Notch for?" and set these switches.

Animations in this mockup

Feature off: pixel crumble

  • The feature's tab breaks into Departure-sized pixels that fall and fade, and the tab bar closes the gap on the shoulders spring.
  • Switching it back on reverses this: the pixels fly back up and reassemble into the tab.
  • The section's rows dim and drift a few points sideways, odd rows left and even rows right.

Switches: squash and spark

  • The knob stretches while it travels, then settles with a little overshoot.
  • Turning one on throws four 1pt sparks. Turning it off is quiet on purpose.

Value fields: slot roll

  • Clicking a value rolls the old one up and out while the new one springs in from below, like a split-flap counter.
  • Section changes slide in the direction of the tab you picked, and the highlight pill glides between tabs.
