hover the notch

A · STREAM

Same move as the end of the onboarding. Pixels pour off the shoulders in curved streams and land row by row, top first.

The most alive of the three, and the one that matches the intro. Each pixel trails a short tail.

B · SCANLINE

The notch builds the panel like a CRT. Pixels spread sideways along the top edge, then drop into place behind a bright scan line that runs top to bottom. Closing runs it in reverse, bottom first.

The most orderly, and the easiest to read through on a quick hover.

C · DUST

The content puffs into a small cloud of dust that drifts, then gets pulled into the new shape on a spring.

The softest and most "liquid". Also the most motion, so it may feel busy on every hover.

How it stays fast: the reveal is done when the open spring settles (about 0.5s). The crisp content is uncovered pixel by pixel as each one lands, so text is never blurry or late. Closing has to be slightly slower than today's 0.32s close, here 0.45s, or the notch shuts over pixels still in the air. In the app: the panel would be rendered to a bitmap once per open (ImageRenderer), sampled into 2pt cells, and the pixels drawn by the existing NotchFX canvas. No new dependencies. Reduce Motion keeps today's plain open.
