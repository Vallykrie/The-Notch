Notch attention motion · prototype v2

Everything happens inside the notch. Onboarding + hook consent first, then the three moments where an agent needs you. Each moment gets its own physical gesture so you can tell them apart without reading: approval pops, a question drips, done breathes out. The cards are clickable. If you ignore them, they nudge again.

NEW · LYRICS PILL

  1. The synced line floats in a black pill under the notch, which drips out of the notch the same way the onboarding hint does.
  2. Each new line glides in. The pill stretches or shrinks to fit it on the lyricsAdvance spring.
  3. When the cursor gets within ~30pt, the pill splashes into droplets that flee the pointer, so you can see what was underneath (here, a browser tab title).
  4. Once the cursor is ~80pt away, the drops flow back, nearest first, and merge into the pill again. The text fades back in.

If you leave the mouse still, a demo cursor brushes past every few seconds.

NEW · MASCOT TRAVELS

  1. Permission, question and done: as the card opens, the shoulder mascot comes apart into pixels that fly down and rebuild it, big, in the card.
  2. On close, the big mascot comes apart and its pixels fly back up and rebuild it, tiny, on the shoulder while the card closes. This is the same move as the end of onboarding.
  3. The cards get a faint star field too, so they feel like part of the same space as onboarding.

ONBOARDING + CONSENT

  1. Notch melts: drops bead off its bottom edge (gooey).
  2. It swallows them and opens into a wide band of drifting pixels.
  3. The pixels fly in and build the mascot. Pop, blink, look around.
  4. Says hi, slides left, and asks to install the hook. Both agent rows can be toggled.
  5. connect: pixels stream from the mascot into each row, which ticks green.
  6. The mascot dissolves, and its own pixels rebuild it, tiny, on the left shoulder while the band closes.
  7. A drop falls off the notch and becomes a “hover to open” pill, then gets drunk back up.

The consent copy says what we touch, that other hooks are kept and that there's a backup. That's the "never clobber" rule, spoken out loud.

PERMISSION · POP

  1. Shoulder mascot startles and turns amber.
  2. Notch inhales (shrinks 16pt, 110ms) and then pops into the card on an under-damped spring.
  3. Two amber rings shock out from the silhouette. Sparks fly off the bottom edge and the rim glows.
  4. Ignored for 3.6s: a nudge (width kick, belly wobble, one ring, mascot hop). It repeats.
  5. allow → card ticks green and closes · deny → head-shake, then closes.

This is the loudest gesture, because it is the one that blocks the agent.

QUESTION · DRIP

  1. A single drop forms under the notch and hangs.
  2. The notch gulps it and grows into the question card. The bottom edge wobbles.
  3. The options stagger in. A “?” bobs above the mascot.
  4. Ignored for 4s: a drop forms again at the bottom edge and is drunk back up.
  5. Click an option, or press 1–3. The row fills yellow and the card closes.

Softer than permission: a question is a conversation, not a gate (the same reasoning as Status.question).

DONE · BREATHE OUT

  1. Shoulders widen, and a green light sweeps along the rim from left to right.
  2. Pixel confetti spills out of the bottom edge onto the desktop. The mascot hops.
  3. The notch peeks open with a summary and a jump-back button.
  4. It auto-closes after 5s. The countdown pauses while you hover.

This one never repeats. Once it's done it goes quiet, and the shoulder stays green.

Motion tokens this would add to Theme.Motion: pop spring(0.42, 0.60) · inhale spring(0.12, 1.0) · band spring(0.60, 0.74) · nudge = velocity kicks on the existing springs (no new curves) · ring 0.7s ease-out · confetti gravity 520pt/s². The existing open, close and resize are reused unchanged. In SwiftUI the goo is a Canvas with .alphaThreshold + .blur (Apple's metaball recipe), so no Metal is needed.
