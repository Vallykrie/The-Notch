Notes panel · three directions

A fifth notch tab for drafting text before it goes somewhere else — most often a prompt for an agent. Each panel is the real 640 × 190 pt panel at 1 px = 1 pt, in Departure Mono at the app's type sizes. The text areas are live: click and type. Everything autosaves; there is no Save button anywhere. The red hatching is the camera housing.

A · Scratchpad

One sheet of paper, always there. Open the tab, the cursor is already blinking. Up to four quick pages (1–4) for when you're juggling two drafts. Nothing to manage, nothing to name. Closest to "write it down on the back of my hand".

C · Prompt composer recommended

Collapsed states + quick capture

Applies to whichever direction you pick. A global hotkey opens the notch straight to Notes with the cursor in the text; Esc closes it and keeps the draft. When the notch is closed, a draft is never lost and never nags.
