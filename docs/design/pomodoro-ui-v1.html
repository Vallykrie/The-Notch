Focus timer (Pomodoro) · three directions

A sixth, opt-in feature: a Pomodoro timer that lives in the notch. It is off by default, so a fresh install or an upgrade never shows the tab until it's switched on in Settings → Focus. Each panel is the real 640 × 190 pt panel at 1 px = 1 pt. All three timers are live and share one clock: press Start in any of them, and use the Speed chip to run 60× faster. The red hatching is the camera housing.

A · Ring

The classic face: a ring made of 48 pixel ticks drains as the minutes go, time in the middle. To the right, which phase you're in, where you are in the set of four, presets, and the controls. Familiar to anyone who has used a Pomodoro app.

B · Big clock recommended

The time is the interface: a 64 pt Departure Mono countdown you can read from across the desk, a row of 25 cells beneath it (one per minute, fills as you go), and an optional "working on" line so the panel says what you're focusing on. The colon dims while paused. It uses the panel's width the way the Media tab does.

Why it might winGlanceable at any zoom, no chart to decode. The "working on" line is the cheapest possible focus aid, and it shows up again in the collapsed notch. Cells give a sense of progress without a ring.
Where it falls shortThe cell row is a fixed count, so a 50-minute focus shows 50 thin cells (still fine at 572 pt wide). Less "cute" than A.

C · Day strip + focus guard

The whole set laid out as one strip: focus, break, focus, break … long break, with the current block filling. Adds the one thing a notch timer can do that a phone timer can't: Hold popups while focusing, which keeps lyrics, media changes and finished-agent celebrations quiet until the break. Approvals and questions from agents still come through, because those block your work.

On the notch (shared by A, B, C)

While a timer runs and the panel is closed, the notch wings show it, the same way Media and Agents show their live activity. When a phase ends, the notch drops open briefly with one button for the next step, and a soft chime plays (can be turned off).

PriorityAn agent approval/question always wins the notch over the timer; the timer comes back in the wings afterwards. Media and the timer share the wings by whichever changed last, like Media and Crypto do today.
KeyboardSpace starts/pauses while the Focus tab is open. No global hotkey in v1.

Settings → Focus (off by default)

A new "Focus" entry in the settings rail, below Notes. The section is headed by its on/off switch like every feature section; while it's off, the rows are dimmed and the Focus tab doesn't exist. Flip the switch to see it come alive. Eight rail entries at 16 pt fit the panel height.

PersistenceA running timer survives the panel closing and the Mac sleeping (it's stored as an end date, not a tick count). Quitting the app stops it. "Done today" resets at midnight.
