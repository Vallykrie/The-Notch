Lyrics: pick a direction

Four directions for the lyrics view, animated live with placeholder lines. Each one keeps the panel's size, the artwork and the pixel font. They differ in how much room lyrics get and how a line arrives.

In every option: no karaoke fill (the sung line is plain white), and a long line wraps onto a second row instead of ending in “…”. Lines wrap at word boundaries, and the layout makes room for the second row.

AFocus: one line, nothing else

Only the line being sung, large and centred in the space the title, artist and scrubber used. A dim preview of the next line sits at the bottom.
