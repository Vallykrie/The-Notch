Focus timer · Kitchen tape + ripening, with Tick

The merge you asked for: F's kitchen tape for setting the time and D's ripening for the running timer. The tomato is gone. In its place is a new mascot, Tick, who fills up with colour as the round goes on. Tick is a sibling of the agent blob, built the same way: a pixel sprite with eyes cut out as holes, shaded in one colour, with a soft glow. Tick is rounder, has a timer knob on top instead of antennae, and has small arms, so Tick can actually do things. Earlier rounds are kept in v1 and v2. Everything below is live.

The panel

Before you start: drag the tape to pull out minutes. Tick watches the needle, and the knob on Tick's head turns as the minutes go by. Press Start (or Space): Tick hops and rings the knob, the tape folds down into a slim strip, and the time grows. Tick sits down at a little keyboard and gets to work, filling with amber from the bottom up as the round goes on. In the last minute Tick types faster and the knob starts rattling. When the round ends Tick celebrates, then has a cup of tea during the break while filling with green. During the long break Tick naps. While paused Tick dims, looks at you and shows "…". Try 120× to see a whole set in about a minute.

Setting the timeThe tape is the only input, with no preset buttons. Drag goes from 1 to 120 min and snaps to whole minutes on release. The length you pick is remembered for next time. Break lengths and rounds per set stay in Settings → Focus.
While it runsThe tape stays as a slim strip that keeps creeping toward zero. It's the precise reading, and Tick is the at-a-glance one. The strip is locked while running, so a stray swipe can't change the time. Reset unlocks it.

On the notch

With the panel closed, a small Tick on the left side of the notch fills up in the same way, with the countdown on the right. When a round ends the notch drops open for a few seconds while Tick celebrates, with one button for what comes next.

Tick's moves

Every state Tick can be in, each looping on its own. Like the agent blob, Tick's outline never changes. What changes is the knob, the eyes, the arms, and how full Tick is. Colour has one meaning: amber is focus, green is a break, and dimmed means paused.
