Focus timer v4 · two-step setup, the agent mascot, three progress styles

What changed from v3:

  • Rulers are for setup only. Set Focus and Break on two rulers. Once you press Start the rulers go away and only the countdown and a progress view are left.
  • Progress, three ways: a day strip, the walk, and a strip with the walker on top. The three panels below share one clock, so you can compare them.
  • The mascot is the agent mascot. Same 12 × 10 sprite, the same antennae, eyes and legs, and the same shading and glow as AgentActivityGlyph. Focus adds props around it (desk, keyboard, monitor, mug), and the blob fills with colour as the round goes on.
  • Typing you can see: two paws tap the keys in turn, each key lights up as it's pressed, the antennae flick with each keystroke, and code appears on the monitor one character per keystroke, with a blinking cursor. Every few seconds the mascot stops to think, looking up at the screen.

The panel

Drag either ruler to set it (Focus 5–90 min, Break 1–30 min). Press Start or Space. The mascot hops, the rulers fade out, and it sits down at the desk. Pause, Skip and Reset sit at the bottom right. Reset brings the rulers back. Use 120× to see a full set of four rounds in about a minute.

Day strip shows the whole set as blocks, focus in amber and breaks in green. The current block fills up, and the end time of the set is on the right. It's the most precise, but also the most like a chart.
The walk turns the set into a path. A small copy of the mascot walks along it while you focus and sits by a campfire at each flag during breaks. It's charming, but with the big typing mascot on the left the mascot appears twice. Strip + walker keeps the strip's precision and puts the walker on top of it.

On the notch

With the panel closed, the left side of the notch shows the same blob the Agents tab uses there, at the same 2 pt cell size. It fills with amber, flicks its antennae as it types, and turns green on a break. The countdown is on the right.

The mascot's moves

Each state looping on its own, at 9 pt cells so you can see every pixel. Amber means focus, green means a break, grey means not started yet, and dimmed means paused.

Left: the agent mascot exactly as the Agents tab draws it (working, in its blue). Right: the focus mascot with no props. The body, antennae, eyes, legs, shading and glow are identical. Only the colour differs, and the body can be partly filled.
